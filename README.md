# 🌸 ADIVYA — Scholarship Success Companion

> **Ask. Understand. Act. Achieve.**

ADIVYA is a privacy-first scholarship companion designed to help students discover relevant scholarship opportunities, understand eligibility, prepare documents, protect applications, recover from missed opportunities, manage renewals, and track their scholarship journey.

---

## 🚀 Problem Statement

Students often struggle with:

- Finding scholarships relevant to their profile
- Understanding complicated eligibility requirements
- Knowing which documents are required
- Missing important deadlines
- Making mistakes during applications
- Managing scholarship documents
- Recovering after missing an opportunity
- Remembering renewal requirements

ADIVYA brings these activities together into one personalized platform.
